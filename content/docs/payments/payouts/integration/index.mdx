---
title: "Payouts integration guide"
sidebarTitle: "Get started"
description: "Fund your payout account, send USDC to a recipient's wallet, and keep your records in step with every transfer."
---

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Fund your payout account

    Find your account's address and send it the USDC your transfers are paid from. See [Fund your account](/payments/payouts/integration/fund-your-account).
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Create a payout

    Send USDC to a recipient with one API call. See [Create a payout](/payments/payouts/integration/create-a-payout).
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Follow it to a final status

    Subscribe to [payout webhooks](/payments/webhook-events/payouts) and back them with a [reconciliation job](/payments/payouts/integration/reconcile).
  </Step>
</Steps>
