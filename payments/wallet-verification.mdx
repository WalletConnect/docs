This feature proves that your customer controls their wallet, before funds move. The customer opens a link, connects the wallet they already use and signs a message. WalletConnect Pay checks the signature against the wallet address and returns a timestamped record to your systems.

A signed message is not a transaction, so no funds move and the customer pays no gas.

## Why Wallet Verification

A wallet address is not a person. It has no name attached and no directory to check it against, and nothing tells you whether the customer who typed it actually controls it. That causes three problems:

- **Self-declaration isn't proof.** A screenshot of a wallet app or a typed address shows that someone knows an address, not that they control it.
- **Mistakes are permanent.** A stablecoin transfer to a mistyped address, or to the right address on the wrong chain, can't be recalled.
- **Microtransaction tests add friction.** Asking a customer to send a small amount means they need funds on the right chain and pay gas, just to prove ownership.

Wallet Verification fixes all three. Only the holder of the private key can sign for an address, so a valid signature is cryptographic proof of control, and the address comes from the connected wallet instead of being typed.

## What you get for each verification

Each verification returns a record to your systems, so retention stays under your control:

- Signature as proof of control
- Wallet address and chain
- Timestamp
- Signed message details, such as domain, URI and nonce
- Connected wallet details

Optionally, the wallet address can be screened against sanctions lists (OFAC, EU, UK and UN) during verification, with the result returned in the same record.

<Note>
  Wallet Verification proves control of a wallet. It is not identity verification and doesn't replace KYC, customer due diligence or your AML program. You remain responsible for determining which regulatory obligations apply to your business.
</Note>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does the customer send a transaction?">
    No. The customer signs a message. No funds move and no gas is needed.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Which wallets are supported?">
    Self-custody wallets compatible with WalletConnect on EVM chains and Solana.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does the customer need to verify again for every payment?">
    No. A wallet is verified once and the result is reused for later payments to or from that wallet.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I need to use WalletConnect Pay payments to use Wallet Verification?">
    No. Wallet Verification can be added to any WalletConnect Pay flow or embedded in your own flow.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
