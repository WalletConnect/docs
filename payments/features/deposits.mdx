Deposits let users top up an account on your platform from the balances they already hold: any asset, on any chain, from the largest network of wallets and funding sources. WalletConnect Pay handles the blockchain, routing and screening, so your platform doesn't need to build that infrastructure or hold user funds.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Your platform creates a payment for the top-up">
    Create a payment for the amount the user wants to deposit and attach your own reference, such as the user's account ID.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The user picks a funding route">
    The user connects a wallet, sends to a deposit address or pays from an exchange balance, with the token and chain of their choice.
  </Step>
  <Step title="WalletConnect Pay screens and routes the deposit">
    The sender is screened before funds move. If the user pays with a different token or chain than the one you receive, WalletConnect Pay routes the funds through swap and bridge providers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Funds settle and your platform credits the account">
    Funds settle in your chosen token and chain, or in fiat to your bank account. Your platform receives the payment data and credits the user's account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Credit accounts automatically

Each deposit is a WalletConnect Pay payment, so your platform tracks it the same way as any other payment. Instant confirmation lets you credit users without manual reconciliation:

- Set a `referenceId` when you create the payment, such as the user's account ID, and match it on your side when the deposit settles.
- Subscribe to [webhooks](/payments/webhooks) to credit the account as soon as the `payment.succeeded` event arrives.
- Review every deposit in the Merchant Dashboard, with the sender address, transaction hash, amount, time and screening result.
