Deposit with Exchange lets customers use the balance they already hold on a centralized exchange, such as Binance, to pay at checkout or to fund an account on a platform, without first withdrawing to a self-custody wallet. The customer approves the transaction in the exchange app they already use and then returns to the flow.

To check which exchanges are available, see [CEX Coverage](https://docs.walletconnect.com/payments/cex-coverage#cex-exchange-integrations).

## Why use Deposit with Exchange?

<Steps>
  <Step title="Instant access to existing funds" icon="bolt">
    Customers pay or top up with funds already on their exchange account. No withdrawal to a self-custody wallet and no extra KYC, since the customer is already verified with their exchange.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reach more customers" icon="users">
    Roughly 90% of crypto holders keep their funds on a centralized exchange. Accepting exchange balances extends reach beyond self-custody wallet users to the wider crypto-holding population.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Direct settlement" icon="building-columns">
    The exchange executes the transaction internally and settles funds directly into the receiving business's own exchange account, with no intermediary transit account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="No network fees" icon="gas-pump">
    The transfer happens inside the exchange, so no onchain transaction is broadcast and no gas fees apply.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Non-custodial and compliant" icon="shield-check">
    WalletConnect Pay never holds, custodies, or moves funds in this flow. The exchange handles KYC, sanctions screening, and Travel Rule obligations end-to-end.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
To accept exchange balances, merchants and platforms onboard directly with each exchange and then activate it in the WalletConnect Pay Merchant Dashboard.
</Note>
